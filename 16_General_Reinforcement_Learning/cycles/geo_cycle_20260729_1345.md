## 2. Analysis: WHY

### A. Why Bing converts at 3.67× base
Bing surfaces our market-metrics pages for Gold Coast property queries. Users arriving via Bing have **higher intent** than Google users (6.9% vs 2.1% conversion rate). Hypothesis: Bing's smaller index means our well-structured market data pages rank HIGHER (less competition), attracting genuinely interested buyers/sellers rather than casual browsers. The two Bing conversions show the full funnel:
1. `/market-metrics/Varsity-Lakes/crash-risk` → AYH → address submit (market data → "how does MY home compare?")
2. `/for-sale` → property pages → AYH → address submit (browsing listings → "what's MY home worth?")

Both followed the exact "what's MY number" law from the reward ledger: address search = 26× lift.

### B. Why ChatGPT is DORMANT
ChatGPT had 2 users in week 24 (early June), then nothing for 7 weeks. All ChatGPT sessions landed on `/market-metrics/Gold-Coast/overview`. Three probable causes:

1. **Sitemap gap (CONFIRMED):** `/market-metrics/Gold-Coast/overview` is NOT in the sitemap. The sitemap has 19,422 URLs but zero Gold-Coast market-metrics pages. ChatGPT's OAI-SearchBot and Bing's indexer both use sitemaps for discovery. If the page fell out of Bing's active index, ChatGPT loses it.

2. **No explicit AI bot permissions in robots.txt:** The current `robots.txt` has `User-agent: * / Allow: /` but does NOT explicitly name GPTBot, OAI-SearchBot, PerplexityBot, or ClaudeBot. While this SHOULD mean "allow all", CDN/WAF layers (Netlify's) can override with bot-specific blocks. Research shows 41% of sites accidentally block AI bots this way.

3. **Content freshness signal:** The overview page has good SSR (46KB, 20K chars, 5 JSON-LD blocks) but the question is whether the `lastmod` signal reaches Bing. Without a sitemap entry, there's no freshness signal at all.

### C. Why Copilot doesn't convert
Copilot shows the most recent AI-chat activity (3 users in W31). All sessions land on `/market-metrics/Gold-Coast/overview` and stop. The page is data-rich but the conversion pathway from market data → "what's MY home worth?" is subtle. Users reading market statistics don't see an obvious bridge to personalised analysis. The two Bing conversions required MULTI-PAGE navigation (market-metrics → for-sale/property → AYH) — Copilot users don't take that second step.

### D. Why we're invisible to Perplexity, Gemini, Claude
Zero sessions from these engines despite having quality content. Likely cause: pure discovery failure. These engines rely on different indices (Perplexity does real-time search, Gemini uses Google). Our market-metrics pages have good Google rankings (290 organic search users) but the AI-specific discovery path (PerplexityBot crawling) may be blocked or untested.
