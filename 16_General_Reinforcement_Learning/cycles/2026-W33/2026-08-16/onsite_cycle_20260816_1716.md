### 2. Fixed the arm grader's false winners — §4

It printed this, and it is the failure the weekly contract was written to stop:

```
onsite_exp_analyseyourhome_1
  street_range  users=114  conv=2  rate=1.8%  lift=1.15×  → leading
  control       users=131  conv=2  rate=1.5%  lift=1.0×   → control
```

Two conversions against two. Nothing separates those arms — the ordering is entirely which arm was
assigned fewer people. The same test called `bridge_compare` "lagging" on 3 conversions against a
control on 3.

Every arm now has to survive one conversion moving: `leading` must still beat control after removing
one of its own conversions, `lagging` must still trail after adding one. In between is the new
verdict `inconclusive_within_one_conversion`, named apart from `inconclusive_need_more_N` because "no
evidence" and "not enough users" are different statements. **No significance test was added** —
directional reads on small batches are how this business runs (Will, 2026-07-29); what is refused is
a verdict no observation supports. Both false verdicts became inconclusive; the genuine signals were
untouched (`discover_mode_v1` scroll 16/732 vs swipe 11/658 still leading; three `for_sale_page_v1`
arms still lagging on 6/6/5 vs 10).

### 3. Fixed a sensor that had never once run — §4

`onsite_friction_signal.py` had reported `0 incidents` on all 8 stored snapshots.
`_hogql()` ended in `except Exception: return []`, and `_deck_dead_ends()` filtered on
`toInt64OrNull(...)`, which HogQL does not have. Every call 400'd; the swallow hid it; the output was
byte-identical to a healthy deck. **Rule 7b applied to a read: the run asserted no outcome, it merely
failed to throw.**

`_hogql` now raises with the status, the API's own error detail and the offending query.
`sections_read` is read as the Float PostHog stores (checked against the project's property types,
not guessed — Rule 8).

Its first genuine run then fired **3 HIGH incidents** — and those were wrong too, which is the part
worth keeping. The "no forward action" test counted only `forward_cta_clicked`, emitted by
`DiscoveryDeck.tsx` / `OffMarketDeck.tsx` — **the V3 decks**. V4 has been the live default since
08-14 and cannot emit it; the event last fired 2026-08-08. So the detector compared V4 readers
against a V4-impossible event, which is a guaranteed incident rather than a measurement. Forward
action is now the union with `offmarket_report_requested` and `offmarket_claim_submitted`. Re-run: 4
users took a forward action across 62 readers over 7d — above threshold, so the deck reads as
engaged-with-an-exit, not a dead end.

## No new experiment served, and why

The mandate says propose and serve 1–3 experiments per cycle on the basis that serving is inert until
the master switch is flipped. **That premise is false and the brief now records it as false**:
`genrl_personalization_v1` is measured `active=True, rollout=[100]`, so anything served renders to
real visitors on the two mounted pages. With that question open with Will, serving a fourth
experiment would publish new copy while its authorisation is unresolved — and it would land on the
two surfaces that get ~2 users/week, where an arm needs roughly 10 months to reach N≥10.

The experiment I actually ran this week is the anchor: a real hypothesis, a shipped change, a stated
kill/scale rule, on the surface that has the traffic.

**Hypothesis.** For a V4 deck reader who has just been given the valuation answer and its ±12% range,
a one-line pointer naming what the written report contains lifts the share of entries that reach the
report section — because the block's problem is depth, not persuasion.

**Kill / scale rule.** Read at ~50 further V4 entries (about a week at 13/day). **SCALE** — consider
the "process decisions" port and a second anchor — if report-section reads clear 15% of entries and
report requests rise. **KILL** the anchor if report reads stay under 8% of entries, or if
`nearby`/`comps` reads fall materially, which would mean the anchor is skipping people past the
comparable-sales evidence rather than pulling them to the report. That second condition is the real
risk of an anchor and it is why I did not move the block.
