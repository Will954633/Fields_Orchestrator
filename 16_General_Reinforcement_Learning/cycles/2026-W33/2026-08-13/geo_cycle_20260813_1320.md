## 3. Analysis — the mechanism

Crawled fine, dropping out of the index, zero impressions. That shape is not a robots/sitemap/discovery fault; it is Bing declining to *serve* us. Two changes land in the window, and both are mine to point at but not to fix:

**(a) The geo-block edge function, shipped 2026-07-21 19:49 — two days before the cliff.** `netlify/edge-functions/geo-block.js` returns **403 + a `noindex` page** to every non-AU/NZ IP, exempting crawlers *by user-agent*. `bingbot` is in the allowlist, so Bingbot itself is fine — and the crawl stats confirm that. But a search engine that spot-checks a URL from an ordinary US IP with an ordinary browser UA gets a 403 noindex page where its crawler got an article. That is cloaking-shaped, and Bing polices it harder than Google does. It also explains the asymmetry: Google's crawler family was explicitly broadened on 07-31 (`[GEO-BLOCK-INSPECTIONTOOL]`) and Google traffic doubled over the same period.

**(b) Two Netlify hard-pauses.** `[NETLIFY-CREDIT-OUTAGE]` 2026-07-23 19:35 — *the same day as the cliff* — and `[SITE-PAUSED-NETLIFY]` 2026-07-29 17:51, the day before AllOtherCodes started climbing. A site that returns nothing while an engine is sampling it can be demoted, and the second outage lines up with the index-shed slope almost exactly.

They are not mutually exclusive; (a) is the better fit for the impression cliff, (b) for the crawl-code spike and the index shed. Either way the remedy touches a site-wide edge function or Netlify config — **uncertain blast radius, so Tier-3**.

Value at stake, honestly stated: Bing was **17 users and 2 of our 10 conversions** in the 623-user window — 2.7% of traffic, 20% of conversions. The conversion share rests on **two conversions** and is directional only. The traffic cliff is not directional: 20 consecutive zero days against ~70 impressions/day.

Copilot needs no separate theory — it is Bing-derived, and it went dark one week later, exactly as the "feed Bing, Copilot follows" model from cycle #4 predicts. That model is now confirmed in the negative direction too.
