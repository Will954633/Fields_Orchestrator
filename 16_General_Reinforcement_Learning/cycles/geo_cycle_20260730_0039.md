## 5. Research Findings (GEO/AEO 2026 tactics)

### Strongest-evidence findings:
1. **Tables extract at 2.1× inline stats** for AI citations (Princeton GEO study, KDD 2024). Our market-metrics pages already use charts/tables — good alignment.
2. **Pages with schema markup get cited 2-3× more by AI engines.** We have 5 JSON-LD blocks — already strong. New: **Speakable schema** signals "cite this passage" to AI engines (Tier 3 — needs implementation).
3. **Front-loading answers in first 200 words** — 55% of AI Overview citations come from top 30% of page. Our pages lead with charts, not text. Tier 3 consideration.
4. **Question-shaped H2s** — 34% more likely to be cited (BrightEdge). Already in the WTA-012 draft.
5. **Freshness: content updated <90 days = 3.1× more likely to be cited.** Monthly pulse already ensures this for market data.
6. **Definitive language gets cited more** (36.2% vs 20.3% for hedged text) — **tension with our editorial rules** (no advice, conditional language, liability protection). We are deliberately hedged. This is a real trade-off: more citations vs. editorial compliance. **Do NOT change** — liability risk > citation gain.
7. **Only 11% citation overlap across engines** — monitoring one platform captures <25% of visibility. We need multi-engine tracking.
8. **llms.txt**: <1% of AI crawlers read it. Mainly used by developer/IDE agents. Low impact, zero cost. Already deployed.
9. **IndexNow**: Pages enter Bing index within minutes. 22% of Bing clicked URLs come from IndexNow. **Key file needed for verification** — deployed this cycle (GEO-009).

### New Tier-3 ideas from research:
- **Speakable schema** on stat blocks — signals "extract this" to AI engines
- **Bing AI Performance check** — Will to review in Bing Webmaster UI
- **Multi-platform AI citation monitoring** — Perplexity, Gemini currently unmeasured
