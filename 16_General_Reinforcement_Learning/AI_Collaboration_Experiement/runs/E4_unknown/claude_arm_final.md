STATUS: VERIFIED — I ran the queries and the join.
IMPACT: Produces three compliant, no-advice, seller-facing statistics that no agency will ever publish about itself: (a) 98 of 322 tracked listings (30%) cut their asking price at least once; (b) median cut 3.29%; (c) median settled 2.5% below first asking price.
