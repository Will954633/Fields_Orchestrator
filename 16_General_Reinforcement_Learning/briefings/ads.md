## 6. Context the agent cannot get from data

- **`03_Facebook/Strategy/krista_video_funnel_scoping.md` is the source of truth** for the
  funnel design, the audience audit, and the resolved conflicts with our do-not-re-test list.
- **Site traffic is ~1:1 with paid spend** — May/Jun 2026 at $0 spend collapsed to ~80
  uniques/month (the organic floor). The funnel only fills while cold prospecting runs — budget
  for *continuous* prospecting, not bursts.
- **Website retargeting audience is ~20 and won't grow** — AU-mobile ITP caps `_fbp` to ~7 days
  and Meta hides sub-1,000 audiences. This is a match-rate problem, NOT a pixel/capture defect
  (that was refuted 2026-09-15). Lead with the **on-platform video-view audiences (~2k)**, which
  match to logged-in FB accounts directly. That is the real reason the video path wins.
- **HOUSING special-ad-category** restricts targeting (no age/gender/detailed; geo minimums;
  lookalikes become special-ad-audiences). Confirm video-view customs are allowed before building.
- The established "sell-focused content is dead" / "broad beats custom" learnings do **not**
  forbid this funnel — they apply at different stages (Krista doc §2).
- Prior data: $1,742 of FB spend produced 0 true-reward seller conversions; the FB **Marketplace**
  listing of an address succeeded where the paid Messenger carousel failed (Will, 2026-08-24) —
  the strongest organic-vs-paid signal this domain has.
