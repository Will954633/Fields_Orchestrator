Emblems — the live set

A single subject on blank paper. Inverted, it dissolves into the deck's ground: no rectangle, no edge, the drawing simply appears in the page. These eight cover 87% of all 18,070 built decks. Each opens with the deck's own theme applied, so this is what the card will show.

Superseded — the v1 scenes

Full-bleed landscapes. They can't be inverted: the sky is the largest area of paper, so inversion turns it into the largest area of ink and the whole thing reads as a photographic negative in a hard rectangle. Kept here only as the reference for why the emblem set exists. Each opens positive; switch paper to dark in the panel to see it fail. Every one of them now has an emblem replacement.

Built by reveals/build_reveal.py from reveals/sources/, gated by reveals/measure_source.py. What decides pass or fail is paper — whether there is blank paper behind the subject — not ink density. A 54% drawing on blank paper inverts fine; a 36% one with a sky behind it does not. Density is shown because it predicts visual weight beside the copy, not correctness.
