## FINDINGS (the point of the dry run)
- **⚠ FINDING A (highest-leverage) — new-suburb pages render THIN (hero only, "1/1 card").** Root cause:
  `getNearbySoldComps` filters `listing_status:"sold"`, but off-market-minted docs are `listing_status:None`
  (cadastral + historical transactions). So a freshly-covered suburb has **no comps for its own pages** →
  the wealth-reveal + capital-gain + market cards all gate off → only the hero renders. In core suburbs there's a
  rich set of `sold`-status docs; a new suburb has ~none.
  **Options (Will decision / cycle 2):** (a) extend the comp query to also use nearby off-market docs'
  `enriched_data.transactions` (recency-filtered) as comps → minted docs become mutually comp-generating; or
  (b) accept hero-only pages initially (they still carry real address + last-sale, likely enough to index) and let
  richness accrue as `sold` data lands. (a) is the lever that makes the whole rollout produce *rich* pages, not thin ones.
- **⚠ FINDING B — refine coverage sequencing:** because of A, minting a *cluster* first (as done — Crusader Way +
  Gershwin Court) is better than scattering, since clustered minted docs can become each other's comps once (a) lands.
  Consider batch-enriching + verifying a whole suburb's richness BEFORE sitemapping it (so indexed pages aren't hero-only).
- **Minor:** "held for just over 1 years" grammar (pre-existing hero template, not this work). Market-pulse absent for
  non-core suburbs → market cards degrade (expected).
