## Analysis

### The bottleneck is menu_sell_rate, not engagement
Post-redesign, 100% of visitors engage with the intent menu. But only 6.1% choose "See how this home might sell today" — the only path that continues on-deck into the value/market/selling cards. Most choose "market" (navigates to /market-metrics — exits the deck) or don't complete.

### Why is "sell" under-chosen?
The hero card currently shows:
1. Address + aerial photo
2. Last recorded sale: $X · Date · held Y years
3. "Hi! We're Fields Real Estate. What would be most useful right now?"
4. Intent menu (sell / market / surprise / else)

The last-sale line shows a PAST number. There's no CURRENT-market context on the hero card. The user sees "$670,000 in 2016" but has no frame for what that means in 2026. Without a gap to close, "market update" (low-commitment, informational) feels safer than "see how this home might sell" (high-commitment, implies selling intent).

### The FB funnel law applies directly
From the Home Owner Lead Funnel (day 1, 21 angles, 6 leads): "specific shocking numbers outperform abstracts 7-10x" and "converting hooks require a personal open loop." The gap between purchase price and current comparable range IS that personal open loop: "$670K in 2016 → nearby homes now sell for $1.4M–$1.87M?! What's MY home worth?" The "sell" option becomes the natural path to answer that question.

## Hypothesis

**Adding a one-line comparable-range teaser on the hero card (between last-sale info and intent menu) will increase menu_sell_rate by creating a personal open loop.**

The teaser shows: "Recent nearby sales: **$X–$Y** · N comparable sales" — factual, editorial-compliant (data-framed, no advice, no prediction), reuses existing comp data (v.rangeLow/rangeHigh).

**Variable changed:** ONE — presence/absence of the comp-range teaser on the hero card. All subsequent cards, menu labels, and deck logic unchanged.

**Kill rule:** No lift in menu_sell_rate after 14 days at N≥30/arm.
**Scale rule:** ≥5pp lift in menu_sell_rate AND downstream meso milestones (forward_cta, qualify) don't drop.
