# Eight sales near your street, weighed against the headlines

You have read that house prices are falling. The eight most recent sales near 20 Heidelberg Circuit, once each is adjusted to your home, and Robina's own recorded median point a different way from the national numbers. This piece sets the two side by side; it does not decide between them for you.

## What the headlines say

The falls are real where they are being measured. Cotality's five-city aggregate fell -0.9% in July 2026 and -2.0% over the July quarter, with Sydney down -3.7%, Melbourne down -3.0% and Brisbane down -0.1% (Cotality, July 2026). The RBA cash rate stands at 4.35%, raised in February, March and May 2026 and held at the 16 June meeting, with the next decision scheduled for 11 August 2026 (RBA). Headline CPI rose 3.8% over the year to June 2026 and the trimmed mean 3.6% (ABS, June quarter 2026). Sentiment has followed: the Westpac–Melbourne Institute House Price Expectations Index fell -8% in July 2026 to a three-year low, though 47% of respondents still expected rises (Westpac–Melbourne Institute, July 2026), and after a softer June-quarter CPI Westpac withdrew, on 30 July 2026, its earlier call for two more hikes to 4.85% (reported 30 July 2026). That is a fair picture of a national market under pressure.

## What sold near you

Eight houses have sold close to yours between 28 September 2025 and 2 July 2026. The nearest is 2 Yarraville Street, 0.19 km away; the most recent is 7 Beaumaris Court, sold 2 July 2026. They are the evidence here, because a sale down the road is a real transaction, not an estimate.

A raw sale price is not directly comparable to your home, though, because no two houses are the same. Take 81 Thorngate Drive, which sold on 10 May 2026 for **$1,410,000** — a real sale price. It sits on 720 sqm against your 477 sqm, so we subtract $97,929 for the extra land. It was less renovated than yours, so we add $86,178. Its internal floor area of 222.65 sqm exceeds your 197 sqm, so we subtract $31,806. Those differences, with several smaller ones, restate that sale as **$1,314,410** — an estimate of what that same buyer would likely have paid for a home like yours. ## What these sales say about your home

Raw, those eight homes sold between $1,200,000 and $1,420,000. Adjusted to your home, they land between **$1,172,211 and $1,380,815** — a range built from eight sales, carrying the confidence grade recorded for this set: high. That spread of roughly $208,000 is the estimate. It is not one number, and it should not be read as one; the width is the honest part, reflecting how the eight homes genuinely differed from yours.

## Do these sales agree with the suburb's own figures?

Split the eight adjusted sales in half by date. The earlier four, from 28 September 2025 to 2 January 2026, average **$1,242,708**. The later four, from 18 March to 2 July 2026, average **$1,315,398**. That is a move of **+5.8%**.

The independently measured Robina rolling 12-month house median moved **+5.8%** year-on-year, computed from 265 recorded sales. These eight sales, adjusted to your one home, moved the same +5.8%. On direction and rough size, they agree — which is the most useful fact on this page.

Now the limits, in the same breath. Each half holds only four sales, which is a very small sample. Two figures matching to the decimal is partly luck; what is worth reporting is that both point up by a similar amount, not the exact number. And this method's own mean absolute error is about 11% in this price range — wider than the +5.8% movement it is describing. The agreement is real; the precision it appears to show is more than four sales can carry.

## Where that leaves the national picture

So the two records point differently. The national aggregate is falling — Cotality has the five-city measure down -2.0% over the July quarter — while Robina's rolling median and these eight adjusted sales both sit +5.8% higher than a year ago. Weekly auction clearances add a further gap of kind rather than degree: the capital-city average was 46.0% for the week ending 5 July 2026, with Brisbane at 23.5% (Cotality), but homes near you sell predominantly by private treaty, so that figure measures a different sale process, not this one. The national and the local are describing different things at different scales, and they are not saying the same thing.

## What this can't tell you

We publish our method's mean absolute error: about 11% in this price range. Four sales sit in each half of the movement above, and eight sit behind the full range — small numbers, stated plainly so you can weigh them. Robina's median carries its own band, a 90% confidence interval of $1,450,000 to $1,550,000 across 265 sales. One street, over ten months, cannot show you a whole market, a turning point, or what any single buyer would do.

Eight real sales near you, adjusted to your home, moved +5.8%; Robina's measured median moved +5.8%; the national aggregate fell. Those are the figures, side by side.
