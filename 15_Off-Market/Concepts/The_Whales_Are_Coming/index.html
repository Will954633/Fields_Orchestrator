The Whales Are Coming

One humpback, one rig, three finishes. Same measured swim kinematics, same articulated flippers, same blink — only the sprites and the ground differ. Each page plays on load and every parameter is a live slider.

Black-and-white sketch

The original tonal drawing, in blue water. This is the base version — the one every other finish is derived from.

Open Video

Ink

Cream strokes on black, matching the V3 reveal deck. The drawing is reduced to an ink-coverage mask and painted in #E6DDD2, so paper becomes ground and only strokes carry light.

Open Video

Watercolour

Palette transferred from a reference painting by luminance-indexed colour lookup, on pale paper. Colour comes from a smooth wash, lightness from the drawing, so every pen stroke survives.
