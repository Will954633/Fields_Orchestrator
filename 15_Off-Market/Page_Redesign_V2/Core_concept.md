However, there are several issues that will materially affect homeowner trust and conversion. The good news: most are system-level fixes, not fundamental changes.

The biggest system improvements I'd make before scaling

1. Does it hit the mark?
The intended psychological journey appears to be:

"You think your home is just another house → actually it has unique characteristics → those characteristics affect buyer demand → here's how buyers see it → here's how we would sell it."

That is exactly the right journey.

The strongest thing here is that it does not start with price.

Most agents:

"Your home is worth $1.8m. Call me."

This:

"Your home occupies a position in the market that most people can't see."

That is a much stronger opening.

The positioning aligns very well with Fields' "Smarter with data" brand.
