# CALLER CARD — keep this beside the phone

Fields Real Estate · outbound calls to Gold Coast homeowners · v1 2026-08-15
Every legal point traces to `00_SCOPING.md`. If something isn't on this card, don't say it.

---

## 1. THE OPENING — all four elements, before anything else

Standard s9(2): name, business, who caused the call, **purpose** — "as soon as the call starts".

| # | Element | Say |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Your given name | "Hi, it's **[given name]**" |
| 2 | Business name | "calling from **Fields Real Estate**" |
| 3 | Who caused the call | "we're the ones who **publish the property data and sales analysis for [suburb]**" |
| 4 | **Purpose** | "I'm calling about **what's been selling on your street** — and if it's useful, to send you the numbers for your own place." |

Then, immediately:

> **"Quick thing first — this call's being recorded so I've got an accurate record. Is that OK with you?"**

**WAIT FOR A YES.** Do not keep talking over it. Do not treat silence as yes.
- **Yes** → say "thanks" and continue. Log consent = YES.
- **No** → "No problem, I'll stop the recording." Stop it, or end the call. Log consent = NO.
- Reason: QLD *Invasion of Privacy Act* **s45(1)** — using/sharing a recording or a transcript is the offence; **s45(2)(a)** all-party consent is the fix. **s49A** puts it on Will personally.

If they ask for our contact details at any point — give them **immediately** (Standard s9(4), s11). Caller ID stays on; our return number is reachable for 30 days (s14(1),(3)).

## 3. STOP MEANS STOP

**Standard s13(1)(b): terminate immediately on ANY indication they want the call to end.**

That includes the soft ones:
- "Not interested." · "Now's not a good time." · "I'm not selling." · "Take me off your list." · a sigh and "yeah, no."

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| "No worries at all — thanks for your time." Hang up. | Rebut. Reframe. "Just one quick thing." |
| Log the outcome | Call back later that week |
| | Try a different number for the same person |

There is **no "5 seconds"**. That 5 seconds is only the abandoned-predictive-dialler carve-out to the *opening disclosure* (s9(3)(b)). Termination is immediate.

**After any request to stop: no contact for 30 days — ACL s75(2).** Mark it in the sheet the same day.

## 4. 🚫 RED LINES — never say these

| Never | Why | Say instead |
|---|---|---|
| **Any price figure, verbally** — "about 1.4", "north of a million", "in the 1.2s", even a range, even "off the top of my head" | POA **s215**: if a would-be seller asks the price and you answer, you must give a full CMA **at that moment** — ≥3 sales, last 6 months, similar, within 5km (Sch 2). Max ≈**$93,000**. | "I won't throw a number at you over the phone — I'd be guessing. I'll send you the actual comparable sales so you can see the working." |
| **"We've got buyers waiting"** / "there's strong demand for your place" / "I've got someone looking on your street" | POA **s212(4)–(5)**: a representation without reasonable grounds **is taken to be misleading**, and **the onus of proving grounds is on us**. | Only a claim you can point to in the data — and log the grounds in the Comments column at the time you say it. No log = don't say it. |
| **Advice on whether to sell** — "now's a good time", "you should list before spring", "I wouldn't wait", "consider selling" | Fields rule: data only, reader draws their own conclusions. Liability. | "That's your call entirely. All I can do is give you what the data says and you make of it what you want." |
| **Forecasts** — "prices will rise/fall", "the market's about to turn", "you've probably peaked" | No predictions, ever. Conditional only. | "Historically, when X has happened, Y has tended to follow. Whether that repeats, I don't know." |
| **Forbidden words** | Brand | ~~stunning~~ ~~nestled~~ ~~boasting~~ ~~rare opportunity~~ ~~robust market~~ |
| **Asking for a listing appointment when another agent's appointment is IN FORCE** | POA Reg **s21** | See §7 |
| **Pressure, second attempts, "just before you go…"** | s13(1)(b) | Stop. |

Number format when reading aloud from the sheet: full figures, not "1.25m". Suburbs capitalised.

## 5. ⭐ "SO WHAT'S MY PLACE WORTH?" — the moment the call is for

This will come up on most calls. **Do not answer with a figure** (s215, above). Answer with the offer:

> "Honestly, the useful answer isn't a number I say down the phone — it's the sales it's based on. What I can do is put together the comparable sales for your place: what actually sold near you, what those homes had, and how yours lines up. I'll email it over, no charge, no obligation. Want me to?"

If yes → **that is the conversion.** Capture: name, email (or postal address), confirm the property address. Nothing else needed on this call.
Note: a CMA given to a seller must not be passed to anyone else without their **written** approval (**s216(6)**).

## 6. "HOW DID YOU GET MY NUMBER?"

**APP 7.6 — they are entitled to ask, and the only honest answer is that we bought it.** Do not dress it up, do not say "public records", do not say "you enquired with us" unless they actually did.

> "Fair question. We licence contact data from a provider called ID4ME and matched it to the address — you never gave it to us. If you'd rather we didn't hold it, say the word and I'll have it removed."

If they gave it to us themselves (Track A — FB lead form or Analyse Your Home): say exactly that, and when.
If they ask us to delete it → treat as a stop (§3) **and** flag for deletion.

---

## 7. "I ALREADY HAVE AN AGENT"

If an appointment **is in force** → **do not solicit an appointment.** POA Reg **s21**.

> "Understood — I'm not going to get in the middle of that, and I won't ask you for anything. If you ever want the raw sales data for the street, it's free on our site."

Then close the call. The 39 "Listing Nearing Expiry" leads are **excluded from round 1** for this reason.
(Expired or withdrawn appointments sit outside s21 — but they are not in round 1 either, so on this round: agent mentioned = wind up.)
