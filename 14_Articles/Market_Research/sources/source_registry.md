# Source registry — reputable sources for market research

Every brief cites from here. Tiered by reliability; primary sources preferred.

## Tier 1 — primary / official (highest)
- **ABS** — Australian Bureau of Statistics. Data API `https://data.api.abs.gov.au`
  (Labour Force 6202.0, Job Vacancies 6354.0, Wage Price Index 6345.0, Monthly
  Household Spending Indicator, Regional Population, Regional Internal Migration).
- **RBA** — Reserve Bank of Australia. Cash rate, Statement on Monetary Policy,
  Financial Stability Review, Chart Pack, speeches. `https://www.rba.gov.au`
- **Australian Treasury** / federal budget — tax & policy (CGT, negative gearing).
- **QGSO** — Queensland Government Statistician's Office (state migration/population).

## Tier 2 — industry data (high, cite methodology)
- **Cotality (formerly CoreLogic)** — Home Value Index, clearance rates, months of
  supply, vendor discounting, days on market.
- **PropTrack / REA** — listings, price indices, sentiment.
- **Domain**, **onthehouse.com.au** (CoreLogic-backed) — listings/sold (our scrapers).
- **Westpac-Melbourne Institute** — consumer sentiment, house price expectations.

## Tier 3 — reputable commentary (attribute as commentary, not fact)
- AFR, ABC News, The Guardian, The Conversation, bank economists (CBA/Westpac/NAB/ANZ),
  named academic economists.

## Internal
- `12_Marketing/01_Research_Articles/` — academic housing papers (Abelson et al. 2005/2023, etc.)
- `knowledge-base/` — ingested document brain.
- Fields' own analyses (e.g. leading-vs-lagging, what-drives-gold-coast-house-prices).

## Rule
Tier 3 commentary is never presented as established fact; it is attributed ("X argues…").
Any claim that cannot be traced to a source here is flagged unverifiable, not printed.
