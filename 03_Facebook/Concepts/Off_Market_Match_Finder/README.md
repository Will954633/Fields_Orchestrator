# The Best House Isn't Even On The Market — Off-Market Buyer Matching

**Hook:** *"The best house is not even on the market."*
**Sub:** *"Can't find your ideal home at the price you want? Fields finds it for you."*

A buyer-side lead concept. Flips the usual seller framing: instead of asking homeowners to
sell, it targets frustrated buyers who can't find the right home on-market, and offers to
source off-market matches and make offers on their behalf.

---

## Message

- *The best house is not even on the market.*
- *Can't find your ideal home at the price you want?*
- *Fields finds it for you.* We find off-market homes that suit exactly what you're looking
  for. We make offers on your behalf.

### Two-step flow
1. **Tell us what you're looking for** — the buyer brief (suburb(s), beds/baths, must-haves,
   target price).
2. **Tell us where to send your best-matching off-market homes** — contact form fill:
   **first name, last name, email, phone number**.

---

## ⚠ Legal blocker — read before ANY build

"We make offers on your behalf" and acting as a buyer-side finder is **agency conduct** and is
legally loaded on the Gold Coast. Per memory:

- **Never describe Fields as a "buyer's agent"** — Property Occupations Act exposure. See
  `fields_buyer_search_whatsapp_concept.md` (this exact framing came up there) and
  `on_market_buyer_research_2026-08.md` (POA s216(3)).
- Acting for a buyer / making offers on their behalf may require the appropriate licence and
  agency agreements. **Confirm the licensing/authority position with Will before this concept
  makes any promise to "make offers on your behalf."**

Until that's resolved, the safe framing is Fields *surfacing/matching* off-market homes to the
buyer (information product), not *representing* them or *transacting* for them. The copy above
uses the stronger claim — treat it as **pending legal sign-off**, not approved.
