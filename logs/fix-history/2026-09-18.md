## [WALK-BUILD-1080P-CROP] Walkthrough build engine crashed on 1080p source — 18:55 AEST
**Symptom:** `03_build.py --stage segments` failed: `Invalid too big or non positive size for width '2160' or height '2160'` — the crop filter tried a 2160² square on a 1920×1080 frame.
**Root cause:** `scripts/03_build.py` hardcoded `RAW_W, RAW_H = 3840, 2160` (DJI 4K) and computed the square crop from those constants. This take was shot at 1080p (1920×1080), so a 2160-tall crop overflowed the frame.
**Fix:** Parametrised `crop_geom(...)` and `grade_vf(...)` to take the SOURCE frame's real dimensions, probed per-clip via `vlib.probe_summary` in `render_segments`. Now handles 4K and 1080p alike (module constants remain as fallback defaults). 1080→512 downscale is plenty sharp for the 512² avatar.
**Files:** 10_Market_Report/Video_Production/Video_Editing_Process/scripts/03_build.py
**Recurrence:** First. Any future non-4K footage would have hit the same wall; now resolution-agnostic.
