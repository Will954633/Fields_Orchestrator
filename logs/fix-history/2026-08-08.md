## [VALUATION-RECEIPT-DOES-NOT-RECONCILE] Shrunk total, unshrunk line items — 09:37 AEST
**Symptom:** On 11 Placid Court the itemised adjustments summed to +$457,605 against a stated net of

**Root cause:** `apply_adjustment_reliability()` scaled `total_adjustment` and `adjusted_price` by

itemisation is the adjustment actually applied. Original values kept as `dollars_unshrunk`.

stored adjusted price on every one.
