## [MI-CASING-DUPLICATES] /market-intelligence/:suburb served every casing variant as a distinct indexable page — 08:40 AEST

**Root cause:** The route accepted the `:suburb` param verbatim, returned 200 for any casing, and self-canonicalised to whatever was requested. **Fix:** Added `canonicalSuburbSegment()` to `src/utils/suburbNormalize.ts` (composes the existing `normalizeSuburbForDb` + `suburbToUrlSegment`) and applied it in the market-intelligence loader, guarded against an empty segment and inherently loop-free. Verified live: all four variants 301 to canonical in one hop; `Burleigh-Waters`, `Varsity-Lakes/overview`, `Robina`, `Gold-Coast` all still 200.
**Caveat found during verification:** the same normalisation was added to `market-metrics.$suburb.tsx` intending a single hop for legacy URLs, but **that loader does not run** — `netlify.toml` line ~143 has an edge redirect that fires ahead of SSR and forwards the raw token. Legacy URLs therefore resolve in TWO hops (correct and loop-free, just not the single hop intended). The route comment was corrected to say so rather than leave code that appears to do something it cannot.

## [VALUATION-RULER-MISMATCH-RETRACTED] Floor-area ruler mismatch is NOT a defect — 08:13 AEST
**Symptom:** I recorded "subject and comparables are measured on different floor-area rulers" as the
largest known remaining valuation defect, on the strength of a 17.8% median metric difference and
the resolver's own docstring warning that mixing them "invalidates premium math".
**Root cause of the wrong claim:** I asserted it before testing it. The metric difference is real;
its effect on accuracy was never measured.
**What the test showed:** instrumented per-comparable floor-area source, n=581. Median 62% of
comparables sit on a different ruler than their subject — and it predicts error at r=+0.027
(|error|) / +0.069 (signed), non-monotonic across bands. Error by subject source: building_fallback
8.5%, legacy_floor_area 9.1%, stated_plan_label 9.5%, all with median error ~0. The adjustment rates
are regressed from the same mixed pool, so the unit error cancels rather than propagating.
**Fix:** Retracted in 16_Valuation/accuracy/2026-08-07-figures.md and corrected at length in the
scoping experiment. Third enrichment hypothesis tested and rejected (after satellite analysis and
photo-derived quality attributes). Real remaining gap: 48.7% of off-market homes resolve to NO floor
area at all.

## [VALUATION-NOISE-FLOOR] Method is precise but not accurate — 08:14 AEST
**Symptom:** Open question since the start of the investigation — how narrow could the band ever be,
and is further work worth doing?
**Measurement:** Leave-one-out INSIDE the comparable set, 4,549 predictions over 581 properties. Our
own comparables agree with each other within ±8.3% (80%), median disagreement 4.01%. Averaging ~8 of
them should give ±3.0%. The method actually delivers ±15.6%.
**Finding:** The band is ~5x wider than internal comparable disagreement explains. The comparables
agree closely with one another and are collectively DISPLACED from the sale price. Random noise
cancels under averaging; this does not, so it is systematic and per-property — either attributes we
capture for nobody (aspect, outlook, slope, frontage, noise) or a comparable-SELECTION defect.
**Consequence:** ~12pp of headroom exists. Reverses the reading that "more data will not help" — that
holds only for the three attributes tested. The 30-home enrichment run should target the worst-error
tail and capture what a valuer sees and we do not, and comparable selection should be tested at the
same time (free — the dumps already carry every comp's breakdown).
