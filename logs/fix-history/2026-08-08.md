## [VALUATION-NOISE-FLOOR] Method is precise but not accurate — 08:14 AEST
**Symptom:** Open question since the start of the investigation — how narrow could the band ever be,
and is further work worth doing?
**Measurement:** Leave-one-out INSIDE the comparable set, 4,549 predictions over 581 properties. Our
own comparables agree with each other within ±8.3% (80%), median disagreement 4.01%. Averaging ~8 of
them should give ±3.0%. The method actually delivers ±15.6%.
**Finding:** The band is ~5x wider than internal comparable disagreement explains. The comparables
agree closely with one another and are collectively DISPLACED from the sale price. Random noise
cancels under averaging; this does not, so it is systematic and per-property — either attributes we
capture for nobody (aspect, outlook, slope, frontage, noise) or a comparable-SELECTION defect.

holds only for the three attributes tested. The 30-home enrichment run should target the worst-error
tail and capture what a valuer sees and we do not, and comparable selection should be tested at the
same time (free — the dumps already carry every comp's breakdown).
