## [BRAIN1-VERIFY-CORPUS-GAP] Publish gate blind to 2,126 units — recurred, now self-reporting — 07:30 AEST

**Symptom:** `brain1_verify.py` — the publish gate that decides whether a brief's quotes are
genuine — indexed only part of the graph. Quotes from **Bothsides Podcast (AU)** (1,670 units) and
**KB:book raw-.txt books** (456 units) cannot be located in an index that never loaded them, so
they are reported `NOT_FOUND`, which is the verifier's own word for **fabricated**. Found while
preparing to verify Brain 1 cold-calling briefs, before any brief was written — not from a bad
brief this time.

**Root cause:** `ANN_FILES` in `brain1_verify.py` is a hardcoded list that must be kept in sync by
hand with `BRAIN1_SOURCES` in `brain_drive_nightly.py` and the `--merge` list in `brain1_graph.py`.
The graph grew to 6 sources; the verifier's list still named 4 of them (plus 2 non-graph ones).
Missing: `/home/fields/brain1_build/Spotify/annotations.jsonl` (u8##### podcasts) and
`/home/fields/brain1_books/annotations.jsonl` (k9#### books).

**This is the second occurrence and the third instance of the class.** 2026-08-10 was the same
defect with `brain1_yt` (2,292 units — every `u9#####` id — causing **41 false fabrications in one
129-quote brief**, headline fidelity 64.0% → 96.1% once fixed). The file already carried a comment
block explaining that incident. **A comment telling the next person to update a list is not a
control** — it failed on the very next corpus addition. Same class as
`[BRAIN1-NIGHTLY-REBUILD-DROPS-SOURCES]` and `[BRAIN1-MERGE-SOURCE-LOSS]`: the corpus grows, a
consumer's hardcoded source list does not, and the consumer exits 0 either way.

**Fix:**
1. Added the two missing annotation files to `ANN_FILES`.
2. **Added the missing control** — `warn_index_gap()`, called from `unit_texts()`. It streams
   `(id, lib)` for all 14,256 graph units out of the 41 MB `package.json` with a chunked regex
   (never holding the file in memory), diffs them against the index actually built, and prints a
   loud block naming **each missing library and its unit count** plus the fix. On full coverage it
   prints one confirming line, so "the check ran" is distinguishable from "the check is silent" —
   Rule 7b applied to a read path: the gate now asserts an outcome instead of merely not throwing.

Verified both directions: current config → `index covers all 14256 graph units`; simulating the
pre-fix list → correctly names `1670 Bothsides Podcast (AU)` and `456 KB:book`.

**Why it matters beyond Brain 1:** Bothsides Podcast is one of only two Australian libraries in
the corpus, and the work in flight is Australian cold-calling research. The gap was biased
precisely toward the most relevant material — as in the 2026-08-09 `[FIELD-NAME-GUESS-FALSE-ABSENCE]`
case, where the safeguard's blind spot lined up with the question being asked.

**Files:** `scripts/samantha/brain1_verify.py`

**Recurrence:** 2nd as `[BRAIN1-VERIFY-CORPUS-GAP]` (first 2026-08-10); ~4th in the

## [DOM-REA-GAP-REOPENED] Days-on-market: two real defects found, REA gap ruled unreconcilable — 12:40 AEST

**Status: TRACE ONLY. Nothing changed yet — the two fixes below are not applied, pending Will's
go-ahead, because they change published numbers on the live site.**

**Symptom:** Will cross-checked the live `/market-intelligence/{suburb}` DOM tiles against
realestate.com.au: Robina 34 vs 33, Varsity Lakes 26 vs 29, Burleigh Waters 29 vs 40. Burleigh
looked like a specific defect. Reopens `[DOM-SYSTEMATICALLY-FAST]` / `[DOM-TRACE-COMPLETE]`
(2026-08-03), which concluded the gap was definitional and stopped there.

**Four parallel investigations. Every inherited hypothesis was refuted, including the framing.**

1. **Median vs mean — not the defect.** `precompute_market_charts.py:417` computes a median; REA
   states a median. Ruled out.

2. **"Burleigh Waters is the outlier" — an artefact of the window.** The site serves
   `precomputed_market_charts.{suburb}_days_on_market → timeline[-1].median_days_on_market`, i.e.
   **ONE CALENDAR QUARTER (2026-Q2, n=15-39, closed 7 weeks ago)**, against REA's trailing 12
   months. On a like-for-like 12-month basis the gap is uniform: Robina 26 vs 33, Varsity 21 vs 29,
   Burleigh 29.5 vs 40. **Robina's apparent match is coincidence** — a single-quarter median has a
   bootstrap 95% CI 20-32 days wide (Robina [20,41]), and its quarterly series swings
   47/35/17/21/20/24/19/24. Publishing one quarter against a 12-month benchmark crowns a different
   "outlier suburb" every quarter.

3. **Relisting — refuted on magnitude.** Multi-campaign sales are 0-2.7% of the pool; recomputing
   first-listing-to-sale moves the median <=2 days everywhere measurable. Caveat: Domain has
   recorded ZERO `Listed - not sold` events since 2025 (Burleigh ratio 0.38 in 2022 -> 0.00 in
   2026), so the live window is blind; bounded instead on 2021-23 where coverage exists.

4. **Date contamination — refuted as a DOM driver.** Switching the window basis from our `sold_date`
   to the timeline's own sale date moves the median 0-1 day. (The detection lag and 11% quarter
   mis-binning remain a real bug for quarterly VOLUME and PRICE — just not for DOM.)

5. **Coverage bias — refuted, and INVERTED since 2026-08-03.** Coverage rose 46% -> 80-87%
   houses-only. The records still missing a DOM are now the **cheap, attached, older** end
   (Burleigh: has-DOM median $1,900,000 / 81% houses; no-DOM $1,195,000 / 33% houses). Top price
   quintile is 82-95% covered. Burleigh's waterfront tail (42 days vs 27) is **88% captured — it is
   inside our median, not missing from it.** The 2026-08-03 "under-capture of the expensive tail"
   explanation is dead.

6. **The onthehouse ingest contributed ZERO DOM records.** `backfill_days_on_market.py` has no
   reference to it. `scraped_data_v2.timeline[].days_on_market` (68% fill on Burleigh sold; fields
   are `event_date`/`event_price`, NOT `date`/`price`) recovers +8 Robina / +1 Varsity / **+0
   Burleigh** — a duplicate of what we hold, not a gap-filler.

**DEFECT 1 (real, live, ours) — the published pool DOUBLE-COUNTS.**
`precompute_market_charts.py` merges three sources with a comment that "dedupe happens downstream
via quarter grouping". **It does not dedupe.** Burleigh Waters: **149 actual house sales in the
window, 245 DOM records in the published pool** — sources A (`scraped_data.property_timeline`) and
C (`listing_status:sold`) are largely the same sales counted twice. Source B
(`Target_Market_Sold_Last_12_Months.time_on_market_days`) is dead at 2.5% fill.
**Consequence: the published series cannot be reproduced from our own database, and it invented a
trend.**

| Burleigh Waters | published | recompute |

| 2025-Q3 | 48 | 24 |
| 2025-Q4 | 54 | 30 |
| 2026-Q1 | 31 | 26 |
| 2026-Q2 | 29 | 28 |

Published history reads 48->54->31->29 (dramatic speeding-up). Actual: 24->30->26->28 (flat).
**The "Burleigh Waters got faster" story is an artefact and is live on the site.** It is also
exactly the Q2 report draft's proposed cover number ("29 days, against 37 a year earlier") — that

**DEFECT 2 (real, live, ours) — single-quarter basis.** n=15-39, 7 weeks stale, and on Burleigh the

## [BRAIN1-VERIFY-BOLD-QUOTES-UNCHECKED] Publish gate read ~none of the brief and passed it — 08:05 AEST

**Symptom:** ran `brain1_verify.py` over the first Brain 1 cold-calling brief. It emitted a clean
run with **no findings and no summary line** — which reads as "nothing wrong". It had in fact
checked almost nothing.

**Root cause:** `parse_pairs()` extracted quoted spans only from `"..."` / `“...”`. But
`brain1_deep.py`'s synthesis renders verbatim corpus material in **bold**, not quotation marks. On
the q2 brief that is **88 bold spans of real quoted material against ~26 quoted pairs** — so the
overwhelming majority of the verbatim content, i.e. exactly the text that could be fabricated or
misattributed, was never looked at. Extraction returned **0 pairs**; after the fix, **98**.

**Why this is the more dangerous of today's two verifier defects.** `[BRAIN1-VERIFY-CORPUS-GAP]`
failed *loudly* — it screamed NOT_FOUND at genuine quotes, so it produced visible false alarms.
This one failed *silently and confidently*: a brief could carry a fabricated quote and the gate
would pass it without comment. A gate that cries wolf gets ignored; a gate that says nothing gets
believed. Same class as Rule 7b — a clean exit that asserted no outcome.

**Fix:** `parse_pairs()` now pairs ids against three carriers of verbatim text on the same line —
quotation marks, `**bold**` spans, and `>` blockquotes — deduped per line.
⚠ **Known, accepted noise:** the synthesis also uses bold for its own emphasis and for labels, so
some extracted "quotes" are the author's prose and will report NOT_FOUND falsely. Guards applied
(min 40 chars, skip label-like spans ending in `:`, skip ALL-CAPS headings), but the residue is
real. **This makes reading the failure list mandatory rather than optional — the headline fidelity
percentage on a brain1_deep brief is now a floor, not a score.** That matches the standing warning
that the verifier scores badly on documents that discuss citations.

**Files:** `scripts/samantha/brain1_verify.py`

**Recurrence:** 1st for this defect. 2nd verifier defect found today; both were found by using the
tool rather than trusting it, and neither was visible from its exit code.

**Not done:** the real fix is for `brain1_deep.py` to emit quoted material in quotation marks (or a
machine-readable citation form) so the gate does not have to infer intent from markdown styling.
Extraction heuristics over prose formatting will keep producing both false positives and misses.

## [SHEET-HEADER-SCHEMA-DRIFT] Marketing Phone Calls header row stale by one column since "Occupant?" was added — 09:05 AEST
**Symptom:** Every label in the "Call List" tab from column I onward described the
wrong column. Column I read "DNC washed" but held the occupancy verdict; column J
read "Property" but held `⛔ NOT WASHED — DO NOT DIAL`; column K read "☎ OUTCOME"
but held machine-written property facts. The outcome dropdown sat on K *and* L, and
column P (Transcript) was hidden from the caller. Found by the layout guard in the
new call panel, not by anything already in the system.

**Root cause:** `ensure_tab()` wrote the header row **only when row 1 was empty** —
deliberately, so a human rename would survive. But the tab is created exactly once,
so the first time a column was *inserted* into `HEADERS` ("Occupant?" at index 8,
16 cols → 17) the header row silently stayed on the old layout while `build_row()`
immediately began writing the new one. Three sibling operations had the same
one-way shape and drifted with it: the column hide only ever *added* a hide (so it
stayed on P when Call ID moved to Q), and `setDataValidation` only ever *added* the
dropdown (so it stayed on K when OUTCOME moved to L). Data was never wrong — every
reader addresses columns via `COL[...]` — but everything a human reads was.

**Impact if it had gone unnoticed:** the caller types outcomes into the column
labelled "☎ OUTCOME" (K), which the nightly run overwrites with property facts —
losing the notes that `sheet_common.py` exists to protect. And the DNC
"DO NOT DIAL" marker was displayed under a header reading "Property", while the
column labelled "DNC washed" showed "not assessed". Caught pre-launch: only
`--preview` rows exist and no outcome had been typed yet, so nothing was lost.

**Fix:** Made all four operations state the negative as well as the positive, so the
layout is self-healing instead of an accumulation of history:
- header rewritten when its width ≠ `len(HEADERS)` (same-width headers still keep a
  human rename, preserving the original intent);
- columns 0..CALL_ID_COL explicitly unhidden, not just Q hidden;
- validation explicitly cleared outside OUTCOME_COL, not just set inside it.
Repaired the live sheet through that same code path. Also corrected stale
`K/L/M` → `L/M/N` and `column P` → `column Q` prose across four files (the docstrings
were written pre-shift and had drifted the same way the sheet had).

**Verified:** header == HEADERS (17 cols); hidden == ['Q']; dropdown == ['L'] only;
re-run is idempotent (rewrite branch does not re-fire).

**Files:** `20_Direct_Phone_Calls/scripts/sheet_common.py`,
`20_Direct_Phone_Calls/scripts/call_list_to_sheet.py`,

## [JUSTCALL-CLICK-TO-CALL] One-click dialling from the Marketing Phone Calls sheet — 09:05 AEST
**Symptom:** No way to dial a row from the worksheet; numbers had to be retyped.
**Root cause:** N/A — new capability. Worth recording why the obvious routes fail:
the JustCall **API cannot place this call** (all 87 endpoints checked; the only
outbound one, `initiate_outbound_call_v21`, makes an *AI Voice Agent* speak to the
person and needs documented prior consent). And a cell link cannot do it either —
Sheets' `HYPERLINK()` permits only http/https/mailto/aim/ftp/gopher/telnet/news, so
`tel:` renders as **unclickable plain text**, failing silently.
**Fix:** Apps Script sidebar (`apps_script/`) using JustCall's dialer **deep link**
(`https://app.justcall.io/dialer?numbers=…&metadata=…`) opened in a popup window, so
the grid stays visible for note-taking. No credential is stored in the spreadsheet.
Compliance gate is enforced at the click, not remembered: the button does not render
unless the row has a real DNC wash date and the moment is inside ACL s73 hours
(Mon–Fri 9–18, Sat 9–17), not a Sunday (Standard s8(1)(e)) and not a national public
holiday (s8(3)); an unknown year demands manual confirmation rather than silently
passing. A layout guard refuses to run if the header row moves — without it the panel
could offer to dial a different person than the one it displays; **that guard is what
found [SHEET-HEADER-SCHEMA-DRIFT] above.**
Also joined `justcall_sync.py` on the deep link's `metadata` (the queue row `_id`,
echoed back by JustCall) instead of only on folded phone digits. The field name on
`/calls` is undocumented, so the code tries a list of keys and **counts** which path
matched (`matched_by_metadata` / `matched_by_phone`) — a wrong guess shows up as a
zero in the heartbeat metrics rather than as a silent fallback.
**Verified:** 30 unit assertions on `toE164`/`prettyPhone`/`colLetter`/`dialerUrl`/
`timeGate` (incl. Sunday, Christmas, Good Friday, window edges) all pass; both files
syntax-check; live layout guard passes. **Not yet verified: the deep link itself** —
it needs Will's logged-in browser and cannot be tested from the VM.
**Files:** `20_Direct_Phone_Calls/apps_script/{Code.gs,Sidebar.html,README.md}`,
`20_Direct_Phone_Calls/scripts/justcall_sync.py`
