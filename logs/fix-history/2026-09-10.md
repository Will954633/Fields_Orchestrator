## [WHALE-OVER-VIDEO] Whale fired mid-walkthrough — video-watching now vetoes all triggers — 22:20 AEST
**Symptom:** Will had the Robina walkthrough playing on /news/robina and "the whales are coming" animation fired over it.
**Root cause:** Every whale trigger measures "not currently reading" (idle past 60% depth, footer linger, scroll reversal, mouse exit). Watching a video — still, no scroll, no mouse — is indistinguishable from disengagement to those signals, and `pageIsBusy()` (the shared veto) knew about focused inputs, open dialogs and body scroll-locks but not video playback. The walkthrough becoming a default fixture today made the collision inevitable.
**Fix:** Two additions to `pageIsBusy()` in `useWhaleTrigger.ts`: (1) `body.wk-active` (guided-walkthrough overlay open, even paused) = busy; (2) any unmuted, playing, non-ended `<video>` with readyState≥2 = busy — covers article films and future embeds sitewide. Muted decorative preview loops stay eligible. `?whale=1` preview bypasses `fire()` so QA is unaffected.
**Verified:** local build + headless pair — control (no video, 72% depth, 26s idle): whale fires; identical run with walkthrough playing: no whale. Control proves the absence is the veto, not a broken trigger.
**Files:** `01_Website/src/components/WhaleMoment/useWhaleTrigger.ts` (commit 8e058cf3c).
