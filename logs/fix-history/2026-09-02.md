## [INTERNAL-OPTOUT-SILENCES-WILL] Will's own clicks never logged — he's opted out, not a button bug — 23:20 AEST
**Symptom:** Will reported clicking the "The market has turned. Has your home? →" button
"multiple times" yet `offmarket_market_article_open` had 0 events in 60d; I wrongly concluded
the button was "rarely reached / placement issue".
**Root cause:** `posthog.ts:283` hard-opts-out any browser flagged internal
(`window.posthog.opt_out_capturing()`), which stops ALL events leaving that browser. Internal =
`fields_internal=1` in localStorage OR host `vm.fieldsestate.com.au` OR referrer via the ops
dashboard (`/ops-hub-4417`). Will is the founder on flagged devices, so his entire sessions emit
nothing. Confirmed in PostHog: 60d `is_internal=true` = 1 event / 1 person vs `false` = 28,241 /
2,398. A live incognito test with `?fields_internal=0` (which clears the flag + calls
`opt_in_capturing`, posthog.ts:231) logged his click cleanly: `offmarket_market_article_open`,
`source=banner_market_turned`, `is_internal=false`, 22:47:30. **The button + its tracking work;
the zero was self-inflicted by the internal opt-out.** Retracted the placement theory.
**Fix:** none needed (documentation/method fix — verify from a non-internal browser before
concluding an event doesn't fire). **Method note:** a zero-count is evidence about who is being
measured, not about the feature (cf. Rule 8 for reads).
**Recurrence:** First.
