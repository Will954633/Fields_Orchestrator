## [MAILER-PRINT-SPEC-BLEED] Pronto print file — 3mm bleed, crops, one 100pp PDF — 11:35 AEST
**Symptom:** Not a defect — John Thwaites (Pronto) replied with his RIP spec and the
artwork we had supplied met none of it: 50 separate A4 PDFs, trim-size only, no bleed,
no marks. Printed as supplied, any cut running 1 mm out shows white on the deep-green
bands that run to all four edges.
**Root cause:** The mailer was authored as an A4 screen document (`@page { size: A4 }`,
`.page` 210×297) and shipped straight to a press. No step in the chain ever asked what
the printer's RIP wanted, so the question only surfaced after John was holding the file.
**Fix:** New `build_pronto_print_pdf.py` — reads `manifest.csv` in row order and lays
every page onto 230×317 mm: trim 210×297, bleed 216×303 (both boxes tagged), crop marks
offset 3 mm / 5 mm long, registration targets mid-edge, all clear of the bleed. One
combined 100-page file, piece n on pages 2n-1 and 2n. Left in RGB — Pronto convert.
The artwork is NOT scaled up to make bleed (that costs 3 mm of design at every edge and
the tightest text on p2 sits 5.6 mm off trim); the outermost 0.5 mm of each edge is
stretched outward instead, which is exact because every edge is flat colour and no photo
reaches one. Uploaded to the Pronto Drive folder; the 19 Aug zip and its READ_ME moved to
`SUPERSEDED_2026-08-19_zip_no_bleed_DO_NOT_PRINT`; new READ_ME answers John's 3 questions.
**Two things the build caught on itself:**
- `show_pdf_page` defaults to `keep_proportion=True`, so the first build scaled each
  0.5 mm bleed strip to fit and centred it, leaving a **white hairline just outside
  trim** — invisible at page zoom, exactly what prints on a 1 mm-off cut. Found by eye
  at 600 dpi, then codified: `bleed_gaps()` compares the colour 2 mm outside trim with
  0.5 mm inside it on all 100 pages and the build refuses on a mismatch (Rule 7b — the
  check was proved against a negative control that reproduces the bug, p98 delta 222).
- QR decode is re-run on the **combined** file, not trusted from the assembler: the
  lay-up is a re-render. 3 of 50 failed at 200/400 dpi and needed a dpi sweep plus a
  crop to the CTA block — a detector limit, not bad artwork, but the script cannot tell
  those apart so it refuses either way. 50/50 verified in the shipped file.
Also verified: 100 pages, order matches manifest row-for-row (address text checked on
every odd page), boxes read back at 230×317 / 216×303 / 210×297, upload size matches
byte-for-byte.
