## [PULSE-FIVE-YEAR-INDEX-MISLABEL] Suburb-compare ranked on index levels, not five-year growth — 07:00 AEST

**Symptom:** The August suburb-compare summaries on `/market-intelligence/:suburb/suburb-compare`
rank the three suburbs on a "five-year index" that is not a five-year growth rate. Live text says
Burleigh Waters' "index gain of 61.5 exceeds Robina's 52.41"; on actual five-year median growth
Burleigh Waters is **last** of the three, not ahead of Robina. The published ranking is inverted.

**Root cause:** `build_data_snapshot()` in `generate_market_pulse.py` emitted a field named
`five_year_index` holding `indexed_series[-20].index_value` — the index *level* as at ~5 years ago,
where the index is cumulative % growth since a Q3 2016 baseline. Two problems:
1. **Mislabel.** The name reads as a five-year growth rate, so summaries compared
   `latest_index - five_year_index` and reported the result as five-year growth. That difference is
   a percentage-*point* gap on a 2016 base, which ranks suburbs differently from actual growth.
   Index deltas put Burleigh Waters top (96.6pp); true 5yr median growth puts it last.
2. **Off-by-one.** `[-20]` is 19 quarters back from the latest, not 20 — Q3 2021 against a Q2 2026
   latest, i.e. 4.75 years, and it landed on a thin quarter where Burleigh Waters swings from
   76.71 (Q2 2021) to 61.50 (Q3 2021).

**Fix:** Replaced the single ambiguous field with two explicitly-named measures —
`index_since_baseline` (+ `index_baseline_period`) for the level, and `five_year_growth_pct`
(+ `five_year_from_period`, `five_year_from_median`, `five_year_basis`) for the rate. Growth is now
computed off `rolling_12m_median_series` with `is_in_progress` entries excluded, so a single thin
quarter can't swing it, falling back to raw quarters only if the rolling series is missing. Indexing
corrected to `[-(20+1)]`. Added the same corrected block to `manual_market_pulse.py --show-data` so a
human writing summaries sees the real number, and amended the suburb-compare prompt to state that the
two measures are different and must not be mixed. Corrected ranking on 12m-rolling medians:
Varsity Lakes +93.9%, Robina +90.4%, Burleigh Waters +73.2%.

**Files:** `scripts/generate_market_pulse.py`, `scripts/manual_market_pulse.py`

**Recurrence:** First. Same failure *class* as [PROPRADAR-MEDIAN-ANCHOR-WRONG] and the March-2026
crash-risk phantom surge — a field whose name implies a different statistic than it holds.

## [PULSE-SPARSE-ROLLING-SERIES] Five-year growth read a sparse series by position — 07:30 AEST

**Symptom:** Caught while verifying the `[PULSE-FIVE-YEAR-INDEX-MISLABEL]` fix: it reported Robina
five-year growth of 129.2% "from Q4 2017" and Varsity Lakes 159.3% "from Q2 2015" — neither five years
before the Q2 2026 latest.

**Root cause:** My own fix assumed `rolling_12m_median_series` is a contiguous quarterly series and
indexed it positionally (`[-21]`). It is not. `precompute_union_prices.py` drops any quarter it could
not recompute on the union basis ("leave it out rather than mix bases"), so after a promote the series
is sparse and irregular — Robina's surviving points run Q3 1991, Q3 1992, Q4 1992, Q2 1993 … with

The original `[-20]` bug was masked because it ran before the promote, on a dense series.

**Fix:** New `scripts/market_series.py` matches the five-year point on the period **label**
(Q2 2026 → Q2 2021) and returns `None` when that exact quarter is absent, rather than substituting a
nearby one. Both `generate_market_pulse.py` and `manual_market_pulse.py --show-data` use it. Corrected
five-year growth, all matched Q2 2021 → Q2 2026: **Varsity Lakes +115.1%, Burleigh Waters +75.4%,
Robina +75.3%** — so Robina and Burleigh Waters are a statistical tie (0.1pp), not the clear ordering
the published prose asserts.

**Files:** `scripts/market_series.py` (new), `scripts/generate_market_pulse.py`,
`scripts/manual_market_pulse.py`

**Recurrence:** First. Lesson: a fix verified only against pre-promote data can be wrong against

## [PALM-PIXEL-REVEAL] Pixel-reveal animation for the Pandanus hatch sketch — 08:05 AEST

**Symptom:** N/A — new concept asset, not a repair.

**Built:** A self-contained pixel-reveal animation in
`15_Off-Market/Concepts/Near_beach_palm_reveal/` that constructs a black-and-white
version of `Hatch_Sketch_Pandanas_Palm.png`. Coarse pixel blocks arrive and resolve
into the fine hatch, so the ink appears to grow rather than fade in. Five reveal
orders (growth / develop / dissolve / rise / sweep), light and dark paper, live
controls, plus a deterministic headless-Chrome renderer for MP4/GIF/stills.

**Two things worth recording, both caught by measuring rather than eyeballing:**
1. The source paper is a flat **254**, not 255, and 71.75% of pixels sit there. A
   percentile-based white point (the obvious first cut) mapped 255→255 and left a
   uniform alpha=1 haze over the entire background. That haze put every "empty"
   block at density 0.0039 against a 0.004 skip threshold — the whole animation was
   one rounding step away from treating 67% of the canvas as ink. Fixed by taking
   the white point as the **modal** bright tone and flattening sub-0.014 ink to zero.
2. The ink bounding box was being set by a single stray speck at y=1279, padding the
   crop with ~100px of dead paper. Fixed by requiring ≥3 inked pixels in a row/column
   before it can define the bbox.

**Design note:** the master is written as an RGBA **alpha mask** (RGB black, alpha =
coverage) rather than black-on-white, so blocks composite onto any paper colour
without painting white squares over the background — that is what makes the dark
theme possible from the same asset.

**Files:** `15_Off-Market/Concepts/Near_beach_palm_reveal/{build_master.py,
reveal.template.html,render_video.js,README.md}` (+ generated `index.html`,
`palm_bw.png`, `palm_ink_alpha.png`, `palm_reveal_growth.{mp4,gif}`,
`palm_reveal_growth_dark.mp4`)

**Recurrence:** First

**Follow-up — 08:20 AEST:** two things found while verifying, both fixed and pushed.
- `getImageData` was called on the mask canvas on every grid rebuild (each pixel-size
  change), which Chrome warns about and which would have dragged the source canvas onto
  the software path — it is only ever *drawn from* after load. Now the alpha channel is
  read back once into a `Uint8Array` at load.
- `render_video.js` leaked its temp frame directory whenever `--stills` was used (the
  early `return` skipped the cleanup) and whenever ffmpeg threw. Temp dir is now only
  created when frames are actually written, and removal moved into a `finally`.
Re-verified in headless Chrome: no console messages, mode/theme switching and backward
scrubbing (the full-rebuild path) all correct.

## [VM-NGINX-TTYD-502] Web terminal 502 + no URL for concept mocks — 08:50 AEST

**Symptom:** Will could not open the near-beach concept page: `404` for
`offmarket_beach_card.html` and a `502 Bad Gateway` in the console. Investigating the 502
turned up a second, larger problem: `https://vm.fieldsestate.com.au/` — the documented Claude
Code terminal — was itself returning 502 for every request.

**Root causes (three separate, all pre-existing except the 404):**
1. **404** — nothing served `15_Off-Market/Concepts`. The VM serves static previews via nginx
   alias blocks (`/preview/`, `/lab/`, `/quarterly/`) and there was no equivalent for concepts,
   so the URL fell through to `location /` (the ttyd proxy).
2. **502 on `/`** — `proxy_pass https://127.0.0.1:7681`, but ttyd runs **plaintext**
   (`/etc/default/ttyd`: `TTYD_OPTIONS="-i lo -p 7681 -O login"` — no `-S`). nginx attempted a
   TLS handshake against a plaintext upstream: `SSL_do_handshake() failed ... wrong version
   number`. ttyd has been up since **Aug 1 08:04**, so the terminal had been down at least a day.
   TLS terminates at nginx; loopback plaintext is correct, so the scheme was the bug.
3. **502 on `/favicon.ico`** — browsers request it on every page; it fell through to the same
   broken proxy and put a red console error on every preview page on the VM.

**Fix:**
- `location /concepts/` → `alias /home/fields/concept-previews/` (symlink to
  `15_Off-Market/Concepts`), `autoindex on`, `no-store`, `X-Robots-Tag: noindex`.
  Audited first — concept mocks and images only, no credentials or owner data.
- `proxy_pass https://` → `http://` for the ttyd upstream (dropped the now-redundant
  `proxy_ssl_verify off`).
- `location = /favicon.ico { return 204; }`.

**Also found and cleaned:** `nginx.conf` does `include /etc/nginx/sites-enabled/*`, and two stale
backups (`fields-vm.bak`, `fields-vm.bak.1784930924`) were sitting in that directory being loaded
as live config — six `conflicting server name ... ignored` warnings on every `nginx -t`. The real
file sorts first so it always won, but any backup naming that sorted earlier would have silently
taken over the whole vhost. Moved to `/etc/nginx/backups/` (outside the glob). `nginx -t` is now
warning-free.

**Verified:** `/` 200 and serves `<title>ttyd - Terminal</title>`; `/ws` returns **101 Switching
Protocols** through nginx with a matching Origin (a 502 without one is `--check-origin` working
as intended, not a fault); `/preview/`, `/lab/`, `/quarterly/` all still 200; the concept page
loads over HTTPS with a clean console and the video plays to completion (byte-range 206 confirmed).

`/etc/nginx/backups/fields-vm.before-proxyfix-*`), `/home/fields/concept-previews/` (new)
