
## [LEADFORM-PH-IDENTIFY-SKIP] Matched leads showed only email in PostHog, not name/phone — 16:45 AEST
**Symptom:** New buyer lead Kathy Davis (jpjonny@bigpond.com) was timestamp-matched to her anonymous PostHog session (distinct_id 01a069c8..., +13s), but searching "Kathy Davis" in PostHog returned nothing — only her email was linked to the person; name/phone were absent.
**Root cause:** Two writes raced. A Netlify function (geoip Ashburn VA = AWS us-east-1) identified the landing at 00:20:47 with email + is_lead + lead_source=five_property_friday but NO name/phone. Then leadform_ph_match.py ran at 00:21:09, saw the person already carried the same email, set `already_ours=True`, and its `ok = True if already_ours else _identify(...)` line SKIPPED _identify entirely — so name/phone were never sent. `posthog_match.identified` was recorded `true` regardless, masking the gap. `already_ours` was conflating "not a conflicting identity" with "nothing to enrich".
**Fix:** (1) match_lead now ALWAYS calls _identify (removed the already_ours skip) — enrichment is idempotent and must run even when the person already carries our email. (2) _identify split its payload: identity fields (email/name/phone/is_lead) stay in `$set` (must overwrite a thinner prior identify); provenance (lead_source, lead_match_method, first_lead_link_at) moved to `$set_once` (first-writer-wins) so we never clobber a richer or higher-certainty source — e.g. downgrading a token-certain identity to timestamp_inferred, or overwriting a specific lead_source with generic fb_lead_ad. Backfilled both existing matched leads (Kathy, Nikolas) — both now show name+phone in PostHog.
