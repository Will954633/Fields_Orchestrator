## [ARTICLE-DATE-HYDRATION-418] formatArticleDate had no timeZone → SSR/client date mismatch — 07:20 AEST
**Symptom:** After swapping the seasonality graphic in article `december-listing-paradox` and bumping its `updated_at` to `2026-09-08T21:08Z`, the live page threw React #418 (hydration text mismatch, `args[]=text`). Console went from 0 → 1 msg; page rendered fine but hydration failed.
