## [DOM-TIMELINE-RECENCY-UNDERCAPTURE] GC days-on-market chart inflated by property_timeline backfill lag — HH:MM AEST
**Symptom:** "How Long Are GC Homes Taking to Sell?" on /news/gold-coast showed houses 3-month median 61d (n=317), while the sibling "Big Cities" chart (same panel/type/window) showed 39d (n=780); Robina per-suburb chart showed 50d. PropRadar & REA said ~30–40d. The two same-page charts contradicted each other.
**Root cause:** The chart sourced from `scraped_data.property_timeline` (+ `listing_status='sold'`), which backfills slowly — a sale only enters a property's timeline when we re-scrape THAT property after Domain posts the sale. Long-campaign (high-DOM) homes are still in our nightly scrape at sale time so they land fast; quick sales leave the market between passes and land months later. Result: the recent trailing window is under-counted AND biased slow. Confirmed via PropRadar (independent, volume roughly flat) and prior-year seasonality (JJA/MAM sold ratio ~0.9–1.0 in 2023–25 vs 0.43 in 2026 = artifact). Not a settlement lag (recent captures were recorded within 1–2 days) and not a real slowdown. The "Big Cities" chart was already correct because it reads `system_monitor.domain_sold` (fresh Domain searchListings). At 12 months both sources agree (~28–30d) because the timeline has backfilled by then.
**Fix:** Rewired `build_gc_dom_by_type.py` to source from `system_monitor.domain_sold` (GC panel via `_gc_panel_slugs`, `dwelling` house/unit, `dom_days`, 3<dom<365 gate, one row per listing_id), keeping the output JSON schema identical (no frontend change). Houses 3m 61→40, unit 3m 47→36; 12m barely moved (28→30). Rebuilt & pushed `gc_dom_by_type.json`. Wrote `docs/DATA_CAPTURE_TWO_PIPELINES.md` (root-cause + diagnostic playbook + guardrail) since this class of error recurs. **Outstanding:** per-suburb DOM (`calculate_days_on_market_data` in precompute_market_charts.py) and recent-quarter sales-volume/price still read the old sources — same defect, next task.
**Files:** scripts/build_gc_dom_by_type.py, Feilds_Website/01_Website/public/data/gc_dom_by_type.json, docs/DATA_CAPTURE_TWO_PIPELINES.md
**Recurrence:** Nth — same family as [monthly_sold_refresh]/[data_source_undercapture] recent-sold under-capture incidents (check: grep -h "^## \[" logs/fix-history/*.md | sed 's/ — .*//' | sort | uniq -d)
