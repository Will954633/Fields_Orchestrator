## [SLICE-COHORT-THRASH-BACKTEST] VM unusable from a 5-way backtest fan-out — per-process reaper blind to cohorts — 11:05 AEST
**Symptom:** "VM is not loading." Load average 41.02 and climbing (41.0 / 27.3 / 15.6 — i.e. minutes old, not hours), 13Gi/15Gi RAM used with 498Mi free, swap 4.9Gi. Root at 87%.
**Distinguishing it from [SLICE-THRASH-MONGO-LIST] (2026-08-24):** superficially the same — the `code-server.service` slice sat at 9.90GB with page cache collapsed to **212,992 bytes (0.2MB)**, the exact thrash signature. But `D`-state count was **0** at sample time, and critically **no single process was anywhere near the reaper ceiling**: the largest in-slice process was ~1.37GB against a 6GB ceiling.
**Root cause:** an agent session (scratchpad `d83d871e`) ran a bash fan-out —
`r() { python3 scripts/valuation_backtest.py … ; } ; r 1500000 2000000 env & r 2000000 2250000 t20 & r 2250000 2500000 t225 & r 2500000 3000000 t25 & r 3000000 6000000 t30 & wait`
— five concurrent `valuation_backtest.py` workers, one per price band, each resident ~1.26–1.37GB. **6.5GB combined, out of 9.67GB of slice anon.** Concurrently, `prewarm_offmarket_artifacts.py --suburbs … --kind market-update --workers 5` (the deliberate mass re-warm logged as `[OFFMARKET-MARKET-UPDATE-MASS-REWARM]`) held 6 `build_owner_article.py` children. ~11 CPU-bound processes on a 2-vCPU box, which is what produced load 41; the memory total is what collapsed the page cache.
**Fix:** killed 3 of the 5 backtest shards (bands 2.25–2.5M, 2.5–3M, 3–6M), keeping the two lowest bands, which then **both ran to completion** (`dmp_env.json` 870KB and `dmp_t20.json` 311KB, logs ending "Backtest complete."). Left the prewarm alone — legitimate in-flight work, and it drained on its own. No reset; uptime (20d) and mongod untouched.
**Result:** load 41.02 → **4.50**; slice anon 9.67GB → 2.71GB; page cache 0.2MB → **1.24GB**; free RAM 498Mi → 5.2Gi, available 2.1Gi → 7.9Gi; D-state 0; workbench 200 in 0.23s externally.
**⚠ Silent-failure trap for the owning session — the `.done` markers lie.** The fan-out is `r() { python3 … > $OUT/dmp_$3.log 2>&1; echo done > $OUT/dmp_$3.done; }`. `echo done` runs unconditionally on the *next* line regardless of python3's exit status, so all three killed shards still have a `dmp_*.done` marker — with a **0-byte `.log` and no `.json` at all**. Anything polling `.done` to decide "all 5 bands finished" will silently conclude on 2 bands' data. Bands 2.25–2.5M, 2.5–3M and 3–6M produced NOTHING and must be re-run. The marker should be gated on exit status (`&& echo done` / `echo $?`), not sequenced after.
**Gap this exposes in [SLICE-HOG-REAPER] (2026-08-24):** `reap_slice_hog()` is deliberately **per-process** — it kills any single process over 6GB (4GB when thrashing). That is exactly the wrong shape for this incident. Five processes at 1.3GB each present an identical slice-level failure (page cache → 0, workbench starved) while every individual process sits comfortably under the ceiling, so the reaper correctly did not fire and could never have fired. Per-process ceilings cannot see cohorts. The missing control is **aggregate**: when `cgroup_pressure()` reports thrashing and no single process exceeds the ceiling, act on the largest *cohort* (processes sharing a command signature, or the newest N in the slice) rather than the largest process. Not implemented — needs a deliberate decision on what it is allowed to kill, since a cohort is often legitimate work.
**Files:** none changed — operational fix only (kill of PIDs 2415345/2415346/2415347).
**Recurrence:** Nth for the slice-thrash class ([SLICE-THRASH-MONGO-LIST] 2026-08-24, ugrep lockups 2026-08-01/06, [VM-WEDGE-PREVENTION] 2026-07-27), but **first by aggregate cohort rather than a single runaway** — a genuinely new shape, and the one the current reaper is structurally unable to catch.
