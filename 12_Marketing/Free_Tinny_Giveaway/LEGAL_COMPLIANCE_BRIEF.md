# Free Tinny Giveaway — Legal Compliance Brief (Queensland)

**Prepared:** 2026-08-27 · **For:** Will Simpson / Fields Real Estate
**Scope:** Running a free "win a tinny" prize draw as a Facebook/Instagram lead-generation
promotion that collects **name, phone and address**, restricted to the Gold Coast / Queensland,
where entrants are later **contacted about real-estate (selling) services**.

> ⚠ **This is a compliance research brief, not legal advice.** It summarises the current law and
> gives a defensible operating model, but the final Terms & Conditions and consent wording should be
> reviewed by a Queensland solicitor before launch — the downside is real (ACL penalties, ACMA fines
> up to **$2.1M** for repeat spam/telemarketing breaches, and Privacy/POA exposure). Sources are
> listed at the end; verify anything material against the primary regulator page at time of launch.

---

## 0. Executive summary — the compliant model in one page

Run it as a **Queensland-only, free-entry, game-of-chance trade promotion**. Then:

1. **No gaming permit is required in Queensland** for a promotional game of chance run to promote a
   business, provided **entry is free** (collecting contact details is *not* a "fee"). This is a
   "Category 4 promotional game" under Queensland's gaming regime. You must still comply with the
   *Charitable and Non-Profit Gaming Act 1999* (Qld) conditions and the *Australian Consumer Law*.
2. **Restrict eligibility to Queensland residents (aged 30+).** This is the single most important
   structural choice: a tinny is almost certainly worth **>$3,000**, and a prize pool over $3,000 that
   reaches **NSW, ACT, NT or SA** can trigger permit/registration obligations in those states.
   QLD-only eligibility keeps you in the one state that needs no permit.
3. **Publish full written Terms & Conditions on a landing page**, linked from the ad *and* the lead
   form, before anyone enters (Meta requires material terms be accessible pre-entry).
4. **Include Meta's disclaimer** — "not sponsored, endorsed, administered by or associated with
   Facebook/Instagram" — and **do not** use share-to-timeline or tag-a-friend entry mechanics (Meta
   prohibits them).
5. **Capture express consent to be contacted about property services** (phone/SMS/email) inside the
   entry form. This one step is what makes the follow-up **calls lawful under the Do Not Call Register
   Act**, the **SMS/email lawful under the Spam Act**, and the **use of the data lawful under the
   Privacy Act**. Without it, the follow-up is illegal.
6. **Run a transparent random draw**, notify the winner, honour a **redraw for unclaimed prizes**
   (a Queensland requirement), and **keep all records for 5 years**.

Everything below is the detail behind those six points.

---

## 1. Promotion type & the Queensland permit position

- The giveaway is a **trade promotion / promotional game of chance**: winner selected at random, run
  to promote Fields' business, **free to enter**.
- **Queensland requires no permit or licence** for this. Businesses may run a **"Category 4 promotional
  game"** (a free-entry game of chance promoting goods/services) **without applying to the Office of
  Liquor and Gaming Regulation (OLGR)**. (Unlike NSW/ACT/NT/SA, which have had permit regimes.)
- You must still comply with the conditions in the **_Charitable and Non-Profit Gaming Act 1999_ (Qld)**
  and its regulation, and with the **Australian Consumer Law**.
- **Queensland-specific conditions that bite:**
  - **Entry must be free** — no purchase and no entry fee. Requiring name/phone/address is permitted
    (personal information is not treated as a "fee").
  - **Random, fair, transparent draw.** Where practicable, allow the draw to be witnessed.
  - **Mandatory redraw of unclaimed prizes.** If a winner can't be contacted/doesn't claim, an
    **unclaimed-prize redraw** must be held — generally **~3 months after the original draw**, unless
    your T&Cs specify an earlier date. Build this into the T&Cs.
  - **Records retained for 5 years** (entries, draw records, winner details, T&Cs).

**Action:** structure as Category 4, free entry, QLD-only. No OLGR application needed — but keep the
records and honour the redraw rule.

---

## 2. Eligibility & geography (why QLD-only matters)

- **Eligibility: Queensland residents, aged 30 or over** (Will's stated targeting). Note a boat prize
  practically implies 18+ regardless (registration/operation); 30+ is fine and narrower.
- **Exclude** Fields' staff, contractors and their immediate families (standard integrity clause).
- **Why QLD-only:**
  - A **national** game of chance with a **prize value over $3,000** can require **permits/registration
    in NSW, ACT, NT and SA**. A tinny will very likely exceed $3,000. Limiting eligibility to QLD
    residents keeps the promotion inside the one jurisdiction that needs no permit.
  - Facebook geo-targeting is imperfect and organic reshares travel — so **eligibility must be limited
    in the Terms & Conditions**, not just in ad targeting. State clearly: *"Open only to residents of
    Queensland aged 30+. Entries from outside Queensland are ineligible."*
- Target the **ad** to the Gold Coast/QLD *and* gate eligibility in the **T&Cs** — belt and braces.

---

## 3. Federal consumer law (Australian Consumer Law)

The ACL (*Competition and Consumer Act 2010* (Cth), Schedule 2) applies nationwide, enforced in
Queensland by the **Office of Fair Trading** and by the ACCC. Requirements:

- **No misleading or deceptive conduct** (s18) and **no false/misleading representations** (s29) —
  about the prize, the odds, the sponsor, or the real purpose. If the genuine intent is to build a
  seller-contact list, the T&Cs must **disclose that entrants may be contacted about property
  services** (see §5). Concealing it risks a misleading-conduct finding.
- **No bait advertising** — the advertised prize must genuinely exist and be awarded.
- **Prize must be genuinely awarded** and described accurately (make/model, features, value,
  trailer/registration, GST treatment, delivery, and whether a cash alternative or substitution is
  offered — state "no cash alternative / substitution at promoter's discretion" if that's the intent).
- **Material terms must be clear and available** before entry.

---

## 4. Meta (Facebook/Instagram) platform rules

Because this runs as a **paid** Facebook/Instagram promotion, Meta's promotion rules apply *in
addition* to the law:

- **Mandatory disclaimer:** the promotion must state it is *"in no way sponsored, endorsed,
  administered by, or associated with Facebook or Instagram."* Put this in the T&Cs and, space
  permitting, the ad/form.
- **Prohibited entry mechanics:** you **cannot** require entrants to **share to their timeline** or
  **tag friends**. Our mechanic (open the lead form → submit details) is compliant.
- **Full official rules must be accessible before entry.** Paid giveaway ads are held to a higher bar:
  material terms must be communicated up-front, with a link to complete rules (eligibility, entry
  method, prize, draw method, winner selection, dispute resolution). Host the T&Cs on a **landing page**
  and link it from both the ad and the Instant Form context card.
- Standard Meta ad policies still apply to the creative.

---

## 5. Privacy Act 1988 & the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs)

Collecting name/phone/address is collecting **personal information**, so the APPs apply.

- **APP 5 — collection notice:** at or before collection, tell entrants **why** you're collecting the
  data, **who** it may be disclosed to, and **how to opt out / access / complain**. The lead form's
  context card + a linked **Privacy Policy** (we have `https://fieldsestate.com.au/privacy`) satisfies
  this.
- **APP 3 — only collect what's reasonably necessary.** Name/phone/address for a prize draw + disclosed
  marketing is defensible.
- **APP 6 — use/disclosure:** you may only use the data for the purpose it was collected for, or a
  purpose the person would reasonably expect. Real-estate marketing is a **secondary purpose** — so it
  must be **disclosed at collection** (and ideally consented to), or it is not a permitted use.
- **APP 7 — direct marketing:** you can direct-market where the person would reasonably expect it and
  you offer an opt-out; where they wouldn't (or the channel is regulated, as with calls/SMS), get
  **consent**. **Entry can itself constitute consent** *provided the T&Cs and privacy policy clearly
  disclose the intended collection, use and disclosure.*
- **Consent standard:** informed, **voluntary**, current and specific.
  - ⚠ **Bundling tension:** making marketing consent a *condition of entry* can be challenged as not
    "voluntary." The gold-standard is an **unbundled, optional** marketing checkbox (entry possible
    without it). The trade-off: an unbundled opt-in yields fewer callable leads.
  - **Recommended middle path:** a **prominent, specific, express consent checkbox** as a condition of
    entry, clearly worded, with an easy opt-out afterwards. This is common practice and defensible for
    a promotion, but have the solicitor confirm the bundling given our real-estate follow-up.

## 6. Do Not Call Register Act 2006 — the follow-up *phone calls*

This is the critical one for "call and follow up re real estate."

- Calling a number on the **Do Not Call Register** for telemarketing is **prohibited without consent**.
- **Consent can be express or inferred**, but **cannot be inferred merely because the number was
  provided.** ACMA **strongly recommends express consent** and expects you to be able to **prove** it —
  *the method it was obtained, the terms that applied, and the date/time.*
- **Inferred consent from a one-off giveaway entry is weak** — inferred consent needs an existing
  relationship and, for a one-off transaction, generally lasts only for that transaction. It will **not**
  reliably cover ongoing real-estate solicitation calls.
- **Therefore: capture EXPRESS consent to phone contact at entry** (checkbox + retained timestamp). With
  express consent you may call DNCR-listed numbers; without it, calling them is a breach.
- Record and retain the consent evidence (Meta lead records carry a timestamp; export and store it).

---

## 7. Spam Act 2003 — the follow-up *SMS and email*

- Any marketing **SMS or email** needs the recipient's **consent** (express or inferred). ACMA again
  recommends **express**. Inferred consent only where there's an existing relationship *relating to the
  subject matter*.
- Every commercial message must **identify the sender** (Fields) and include a **functional, easy
  unsubscribe**.
- Penalties are significant (up to **$220,000 per breach**, higher for repeat conduct) — enforced by
  ACMA.
- **Therefore:** the same express-consent checkbox should cover **phone, SMS and email**; every message
  carries sender ID + unsubscribe (our `lead_sms_responder` already offers STOP — verify it identifies
  Fields and honours opt-outs).

---

## 8. Property Occupations Act 2014 (Qld) — the real-estate follow-up itself

The giveaway is not agent conduct — but the **downstream call soliciting a listing is.** Flag for the
solicitor / ensure the sales process complies:

- Soliciting a seller and acting on a sale is **licensed real-estate agent activity**; a proper
  **client appointment (Form 6)** is required *before* acting for a seller, and POA conduct rules apply
  (disclosure of any beneficial interest, no misrepresentation, commission/appointment formalities).
- Keep the **giveaway** (prize draw) and the **real-estate engagement** (listing) as clearly separate
  steps: win/enter → consented contact → *then* a compliant agent appointment if they want to proceed.
- This is consistent with our existing legal notes on direct contact and POA — treat the follow-up
  script as a separate compliance item.

## 9. Terms & Conditions — mandatory contents checklist

Host on a landing page; link from ad + Instant Form. Include:

- [ ] **Promoter** identity + ABN + contact address (Fields Real Estate).
- [ ] **Eligibility:** QLD residents aged 30+; staff/family excluded; one entry per person (state rule).
- [ ] **How to enter** (submit the Facebook/Instagram lead form with name, phone, address) and that
      **entry is free**.
- [ ] **Promotion period:** start and end date/time (with timezone, AEST).
- [ ] **Prize:** description, make/model, value (inc GST treatment), what's included (trailer/rego?),
      delivery, **no cash alternative / substitution** clause, transfer rules.
- [ ] **Draw:** date, time, place, **random** method; witnessed where practicable.
- [ ] **Winner notification** (how/when) and **publication** (only with consent / as required).
- [ ] **Unclaimed-prize redraw** clause (QLD) — date and method.
- [ ] **Privacy statement:** what's collected, why, who it's shared with, link to Privacy Policy, how to
      opt out/access/complain (APP 5).
- [ ] **Marketing consent statement** — that entrants consent to Fields contacting them by phone, SMS
      and email about property services and the promotion (see §10 wording).
- [ ] **Meta disclaimer** — not sponsored/endorsed/administered by Facebook or Instagram.
- [ ] **ACL-safe language** — no misleading claims about odds/prize; limitation of liability to the
      extent permitted by the ACL (cannot exclude non-excludable guarantees).
- [ ] **Governing law:** Queensland.

---

## 10. Consent mechanics (what goes in the Instant Form)

The Meta Instant Form should carry:

1. **Prefilled fields:** First name, Phone (auto-prefill), plus **Address** (custom field — required, as
   the prize is delivered / eligibility depends on QLD residency).
2. **A context card** stating: the prize, that it's a free QLD-only draw for 30+, a **link to full
   T&Cs**, the **Meta disclaimer**, and a short **privacy line**.
3. **An express-consent question** (custom "consent" checkbox), worded clearly, e.g.:

   > ☑ *"I'm a Queensland resident aged 30+. I've read the [Terms & Conditions] and [Privacy Policy].
   > I consent to Fields contacting me by phone, SMS and email about property services and this
   > promotion. I can opt out anytime."*

   This single, specific, retained consent is what satisfies **DNCR (calls) + Spam Act (SMS/email) +
   Privacy Act (use/disclosure)** simultaneously. Export and store the consent + timestamp for 5 years.

4. **Thank-you screen:** confirm entry, restate the draw date, and (optionally) the address/analysis
   second step.

## 11. Operational checklist & record-keeping

- [ ] T&Cs drafted and **reviewed by a QLD solicitor**; hosted at a stable URL.
- [ ] Ad targeted to QLD/Gold Coast; **eligibility gated in T&Cs** to QLD 30+.
- [ ] Instant Form: name + phone (prefill) + address + **express consent checkbox** + T&C/Privacy links.
- [ ] Meta disclaimer present; **no share/tag mechanics**.
- [ ] Draw process defined (date, random method, witness); **redraw** clause set.
- [ ] Winner notification + publication (consented) process.
- [ ] **Records kept 5 years:** entries, consents (with timestamps), draw records, winner details, the
      exact T&Cs version shown.
- [ ] Follow-up messaging carries **sender ID + unsubscribe/STOP**; suppress anyone who opts out.
- [ ] Real-estate follow-up uses a **POA-compliant** script and a proper client appointment before
      acting for any seller.

## 12. Key risks & recommendations

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Prize >$3,000 reaches other states → interstate permit obligations | **QLD-only eligibility** in T&Cs; QLD/GC ad targeting |
| Calling entrants breaches Do Not Call Register | **Express phone-consent checkbox** at entry; retain proof |
| SMS/email breaches Spam Act (fines to $2.1M) | Same express consent; sender ID + unsubscribe on every message |
| Real purpose (seller leads) undisclosed → misleading conduct / Privacy breach | **Disclose the marketing purpose** in T&Cs + consent |
| Consent challenged as bundled/involuntary | Prominent, specific, express wording; solicitor sign-off; consider optional opt-in |
| Meta takedown | Disclaimer + no share/tag + full rules linked pre-entry |
| Follow-up = unlicensed agent conduct | POA-compliant process; Form 6 before acting for a seller |

**Bottom line:** the promotion is **entirely runnable in Queensland with no gaming permit**, provided it
is **free-entry, QLD-only, fully documented in T&Cs, Meta-compliant, and — critically — carries an
express consent to phone/SMS/email property marketing captured at entry.** Get a QLD solicitor to
review the final T&Cs and consent wording; everything else is process we can build.
